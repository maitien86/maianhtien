Research & Publications

Research Overview

Understanding human preferences and predicting human behavior in complex and interactive environments has been a longstanding focus across econometrics, social science, and machine learning. My long-term aim is to address how we can efficiently model and predict human behavior in complex, dynamic, and interactive environments; make the modeling adaptive and robust; and ensure these behavioral models are useful for downstream decision-making tasks.

Methodologies

Discrete choice theory Robust/Stochastic optimization Dynamic programming Reinforcement / Imitation learning Simulation-based optimization

Applications

Behavior modeling Transportation planning Revenue/workforce management AI / Security Games
